About & Process

How I work

I design for people,
not screens.

With 5+ years of experience across SaaS products, eGovernance platforms and digital media, I've developed a deep belief that the best design is invisible — it simply works.

My background in Visual Communication and Computer Science gives me a rare combination: I understand both aesthetics and technical constraints. This lets me design solutions that are not just beautiful, but actually buildable.

I'm also actively integrating AI-powered research methods into my workflow — using generative AI to synthesise research faster, generate sharper personas, and uncover insights that traditional methods might miss.

AI-Powered UX Research
I use generative AI tools and prompt engineering to accelerate user research synthesis, generate data-driven personas, and surface deeper insights — delivering smarter design decisions in less time.

My Design Process

01
Discover & Research
User interviews, surveys, heuristic evaluation, and AI-powered synthesis to deeply understand user goals and pain points.
02
Define & Strategise
Personas, journey maps, and information architecture to frame the problem and align stakeholders on direction.
03
Design & Prototype
Wireframes, interaction design and high-fidelity prototypes in Figma — iterating rapidly based on feedback.
04
Test & Validate
Usability testing with real users, measuring task completion, error rates, and satisfaction scores.
05
Deliver & Iterate
Developer handoff with design tokens and specs, followed by continuous improvement based on live data.
